FITPLUS WELLNESS & HEALTH CLUB
ANNUAL MEMBERSHIP AGREEMENT AND TERMS OF SERVICE

This Membership Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on this 1st day of October, 2026, by and between FitPlus Wellness Private Limited, operating under the brand name "FitPlus Fitness Club", having its registered office at Koramangala 4th Block, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560034 (hereinafter referred to as the "Club"), and the undersigned Member (hereinafter referred to as the "Member").

1. MEMBERSHIP ENROLMENT AND CHARGES
The Member agrees to subscribe to the Annual Tier-1 Platinum Fitness Plan. The annual membership fee is fixed at INR 24,000 (Rupees Twenty Four Thousand only) for the initial 12-month billing period. In addition, an initial joining and administrative onboarding fee of INR 3,500 shall be collected upon signature. The Member expressly acknowledges and agrees that the joining fee and all subscription fees paid are non-refundable under any circumstances whatsoever, regardless of relocation, medical condition, club closure, or non-utilization of club amenities.

2. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL AND EVERGREEN CLAUSE
Upon expiration of the initial twelve (12) month term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive consecutive terms of twelve (12) months each at the prevailing annual membership rate, unless the Member terminates this Agreement strictly in accordance with Clause 3 below. The Member hereby authorizes the Club to auto-debit the linked credit card or bank account for each renewal term without requiring further consent or notification.

3. NOTICE PERIOD AND MANDATORY PROCEDURE FOR CANCELLATION
If the Member elects not to renew their membership, the Member must provide formal written notice of non-renewal exactly sixty (60) days before renewal date of the then-current membership period. Furthermore, notice of cancellation must be submitted exclusively via physical Registered Post or Speed Post addressed to the Club Secretary at the registered office address. Cancellation requests submitted via electronic mail, mobile application, telephonic conversation, or in person at the facility reception desk shall be deemed invalid and shall not halt automatic renewal.

4. UNILATERAL REVISION OF CHARGES AND SERVICES
The Club reserves the right to change, modify, increase membership fees, alter opening hours, cancel group classes, or replace training amenities at any time in its sole discretion without prior notice to the Member. Any adjusted membership fees shall become effective immediately and shall apply to subsequent recurring charges.

By executing this Agreement, the Member grants explicit consent to the Club to collect, retain, analyze, and share personal data, contact information, biometric health metrics, and emergency details with commercial banking partners, third-party insurance providers, and advertising affiliates for promotional and marketing purposes.

The Member utilizes all gymnasium equipment, steam rooms, swimming pools, and physical training zones at their own risk. The Club, its directors, trainers, and staff shall not be liable for any personal injury, permanent impairment, loss of personal property, or demise occurring on the club premises. The Member agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Club against any third-party claims arising from Member's presence or actions.

7. FACILITY SUSPENSION AND DEFAULT
The Club reserves the unrestricted right to suspend or terminate the Member's access without refund if the Member fails to comply with staff directives or gym etiquette. Any overdue account balances shall attract late payment charges of INR 500 per week plus 18% GST until fully settled.
