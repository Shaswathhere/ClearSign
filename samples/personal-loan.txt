Dear Customer,
We are pleased to communicate the in-principle sanction of your Unsecured Personal Loan Facility subject to the terms and conditions outlined hereunder.

1. PRINCIPAL LOAN AMOUNT AND DISBURSEMENT
The total sanctioned loan facility principal amount is INR 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh only) repayable over a tenure of 36 months in equated monthly installments (EMIs). An upfront non-refundable processing fee of 2.5% on the gross loan principal amount plus applicable GST shall be deducted directly from the loan disbursement.

2. FLOATING INTEREST RATE AND BENCHMARK RESET
The loan facility is sanctioned at an initial benchmark rate of 14.5% per annum on a monthly reducing balance. The Member explicitly acknowledges that this is a variable and floating interest rate, which is floating rate subject to change and periodic review at the sole discretion of the Lender without prior individual notice based on changes in Repo Rate or Cost of Funds. Any rate upward reset may result in increased EMI amount or extended tenure.

3. REPAYMENT AND EQUATED MONTHLY INSTALLMENTS
The borrower shall service the monthly EMI on or before the 5th day of every calendar month through automated National Automated Clearing House (NACH) mandate. In the event of default or bounced installment mandate, a penal interest charge of 24% per annum compounded monthly shall be levied on all overdue sums until regularized, along with a mandate return charge of INR 750 per bounce.

4. PREPAYMENT, FORECLOSURE AND EARLY CLOSURE PENALTIES
No part-prepayment or full foreclosure of the loan facility is permitted within the first six (6) months of disbursement. Post 6 months, early closure is permissible subject to a prepayment penalty fee of 4% on the total outstanding principal balance at the time of premature termination plus applicable statutory taxes.

5. CREDIT INFORMATION, CONSENT AND DATA DISCLOSURE
The Borrower grants unconditional consent and authorization to the Lender to share all personal, financial, biometric, repayment, and demographic data with credit information bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark), collection agents, telemarketing entities, insurance intermediaries, and third-party corporate partners for cross-selling financial products.

6. JURISDICTION AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes, controversies, or claims arising out of this Sanction Letter shall be referred to sole arbitration appointed exclusively by the Lender under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Mumbai, and courts in Mumbai shall hold exclusive jurisdiction.
