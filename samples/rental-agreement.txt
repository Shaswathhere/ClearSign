RESIDENTIAL LEASE AND TENANCY AGREEMENT

This Residential Rental Agreement is made and executed on this 15th day of November, 2026, at Indiranagar, Bengaluru, Karnataka, by and between:

Mr. R. Sundararajan, residing at Flat 402, Green Meadows, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038 (hereinafter referred to as the "LESSOR" / "Landlord", which expression shall include his legal heirs, executors, and assigns) of the ONE PART;
AND
Ms. Priya Sharma, daughter of Shri Alok Sharma, presently employed at Bengaluru (hereinafter referred to as the "LESSEE" / "Tenant", which expression shall include her successors and permitted assigns) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute lawful owner of the residential premises situated at Flat 201, Second Floor, Palm Heights, 12th Main, HAL 2nd Stage, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560008, comprising a 2-BHK apartment with attached fixtures ("Scheduled Premises").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. TENANCY TENURE AND MANDATORY LOCK-IN PERIOD
The lease shall be valid for a tenure of eleven (11) calendar months commencing from 1st December 2026 to 31st October 2027. Both parties mutually agree that there shall be an absolute mandatory lock-in period of six (6) months from the commencement date. If the Tenant vacates or terminates tenancy prior to the expiry of the 6-month lock-in period, the entire security deposit shall be forfeited and the Tenant shall remain liable to pay the full monthly rent for the remainder of the lock-in period as liquidated damages.

2. MONTHLY RENT AND PERIODIC ESCALATION
The monthly rent payable by the Tenant for the Scheduled Premises is INR 35,000 (Rupees Thirty Five Thousand only) per month, payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar month. In the event this agreement is renewed or extended beyond 11 months, the monthly rent shall automatically increase by 10% on the prevailing rate.

3. INTEREST-FREE REFUNDABLE SECURITY DEPOSIT AND DISCRETIONARY DEDUCTIONS
The Tenant has deposited an interest-free security deposit of INR 2,10,000 (Rupees Two Lakh Ten Thousand only) with the Landlord. Upon termination of tenancy and handover of vacant possession, the deposit shall be returned within 45 days. Provided however, all painting charges, restoration costs, damages, and cleaning charges shall be deducted at landlord's sole discretion without requirement of invoices, receipts, or joint inspection.

4. NOTICE PERIOD FOR VACATING POST LOCK-IN
Subsequent to the completion of the mandatory lock-in period, either party may terminate this agreement by serving two (2) months prior written notice to the other party. If the Tenant fails to deliver two months notice, two months rent shall be deducted from the security deposit in lieu thereof.

5. INSPECTION AND UNRESTRICTED LANDLORD ENTRY
The Landlord and authorized agents shall have the full right to enter, inspect, show the premises to prospective purchasers or contractors at any time of day or night without prior notice to the Tenant. The Tenant shall not restrict access or demand appointment scheduling.

6. UTILITIES, MAINTENANCE AND LATE CHARGES
Electricity bills, water consumption, and apartment society maintenance charges of INR 4,500 per month shall be paid directly by the Tenant. Any delay in monthly rent payment beyond the 5th day shall attract late payment charges of INR 250 per day of delay.
